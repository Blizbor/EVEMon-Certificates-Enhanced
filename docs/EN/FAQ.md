# FAQ

## Can I use this together with Pilot Progression?
Yes.

## Does the patch change `eve-skills`?
No.

## Can Ship Mastery results change?
Yes. Default certificate requirements are changed, so their results can affect mastery display. The `eve-masteries` datafile itself is not patched.
